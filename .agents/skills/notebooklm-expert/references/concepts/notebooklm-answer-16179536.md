# Report a problem

If you get a response that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can give feedback on that response to let us know.  If you have a general issue with NotebookLM or just want to share your thoughts on NotebookLM, you can report a problem.  Your feedback helps us make NotebookLM better for everyone. When you submit feedback, the associated content is shared and might be reviewed by a human, see below for details

**You can provide feedback or report a problem with NotebookLM in multiple ways.**

1. On your computer, open [NotebookLM](http://notebooklm.google.com).
2. Select Settings ![settings](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/G0fDsUsfss4SjL1AUNcvOAQm6em6mY4nze9I) ![and then](//lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36)Send Feedback ![](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/vU68AlUPdq8hSq4RrbGWqeJZQt4NdotTVdkX).
3. Enter your feedback. You can choose to add a screenshot to help us better understand the issue.
4. At the bottom, click **Send**.

## Report a shared notebook

If you find content in a shared notebook that you believe violates the law, let us know. We'll review the material and consider blocking, limiting, or removing access to it.

To report this content:

1. Open a notebook.
2. Go to **Settings** ![and then](//lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) **Report notebook**.

**Tip:** You can also report through [Report Content On Google](/legal/troubleshooter/1114905?product=notebooklm#ts=1115658%2C13774968%2C14314233).

## Report public notebooks

You can report public notebooks that you consider to contain a violation of Google's [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms), including the [Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy). Violations include:

* Spam, malware, and phishing
* Violence
* Hate speech
* Terrorist content
* Harassment, bullying, and threats
* Sexually explicit material
* Illegal activities

To report a violation:

1. Open a notebook.
2. Go to Settings ![and then](//lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) Report notebook.
3. Choose the type of abuse found in the file. Each abuse type has a description to help you determine if the file has violated our policies.
4. Click **Submit Abuse Report**.

Reports don't guarantee removal of the file or any other action on Google's part. Content that you disagree with or deem inappropriate is not always a violation of Google's Terms of Service or program policies.

Actions we may take on abusive material:

* Deleting the notebook
* Suspending access to the notebook
* Limiting who can view the notebook
* Report illegal materials to appropriate law enforcement authorities

## What happens when you send feedback

When you share thumbs up or thumbs down feedback, we’ll collect the associated content – like your prompts, customizations, sources, uploads, and the outputs received (e.g., chats, audio overviews, video overviews, reports, mind maps, source discovery, etc.) – so we can understand the issue. This data is handled in line with our [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and is used to help us improve the product for everyone.

## How Google uses your feedback

Your thumbs up or down feedback, any included content (e.g., your queries, uploads, model responses, generated outputs like video overviews, audio overviews, chats, source discovery, etc.), and related data are:

* **Reviewed by specially trained teams**. Human review is necessary to help identify, address, and report potential problems raised in feedback. In some cases, this is required by law. We take steps to protect your privacy as part of this process. This includes disconnecting your feedback from your Google Account before reviewers see or annotate them. **Please help us by not including any confidential or sensitive information in your feedback**.
* **Used consistent with the Google Privacy Policy**. Google uses this data to provide, improve, and develop Google products, services, and machine-learning technologies.  This is explained in more detail in our [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  + For example, we use this data to make NotebookLM safer. It helps us detect and avoid unsafe requests or responses in the future.
* **Retained for up to 3 years**. Reviewed feedback, included content, and related data are retained for up to 3 years, disconnected from your Google Account.
* **Google Workspace or Google Workspace for Education users**. Your uploads, queries and the model's responses in NotebookLM will not be reviewed by human reviewers even when you provide thumbs up or down feedback, and will not be used to train AI models.
