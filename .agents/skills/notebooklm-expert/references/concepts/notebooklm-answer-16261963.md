# Change output language in NotebookLM

You can now select your output language in NotebookLM. Your study guides, documentation, Audio Overviews, and chat responses will use your chosen language.

1. On your computer, open [NotebookLM](http://notebooklm.google.com).
2. At the top right, select Settings ![Settings icon](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/E3D199A7348F5C1C1B50D30218105251CEF2) ![and then](//lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) Output Language ![](//lh3.googleusercontent.com/QuG2tzBaHqYETEGsu_zfvU23MZI_L2qe7FQu1tLTP4aFS_H0_Vg1BJ9z2CIpDhrjuCMu=w36).
3. Select your preferred language for generated text.

**Tip:** NotebookLM uses the preferred language from your [Google Account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/language?pli=1) as your default Output Language.
