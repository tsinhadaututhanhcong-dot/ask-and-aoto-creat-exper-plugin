# Join the NotebookLM community

To share your experiences and provide valuable feedback, you can join the NotebookLM community on the Discord server. Connect with other users, share your use cases, and help us improve by reporting any issues you find.

**Important:** Safari doesn’t support web technologies that Discord uses. You may use Google Chrome or another browser instead.

To join the server:

1. Go to the [invite link](https://discord.com/invite/notebooklm).
2. To join, select **Accept Invite**.

**Tip:** Make sure you’re signed in to your Discord account.
