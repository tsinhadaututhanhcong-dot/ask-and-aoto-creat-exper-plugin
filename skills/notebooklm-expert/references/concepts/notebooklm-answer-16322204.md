# Use public notebooks and featured notebooks in NotebookLM - NotebookLM Help
**Source:** [https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16322204?hl=en&amp;ref_topic=16164070](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16322204?hl=en&amp;ref_topic=16164070)

# Use public notebooks and featured notebooks in NotebookLM

## Public Notebooks

You can make your notebook public and share a link with anyone with a Google account. Public sharing makes NotebookLM an even more powerful tool for exploration, understanding, and creation.

### How to share a Public Notebook

1. Create a new notebook or open an existing notebook.
2. Select the “Share” button in the top-right corner of your notebook.
3. Set the access to “Anyone with a link.”
4. Copy and share the link to anyone with a Google account to have them view your notebook.
   * Copy the link and share it with recipients.  You can also choose to “Copy link to chat view” to copy the Chat View link.
   * **Note, viewers may still be able to access sources and artifacts.** Chat View hides these materials for a focused experience, but it does not completely revoke a viewer's underlying access to the notebook contents. Viewers may still find ways to navigate to the hidden materials outside of the default chat experience.

Owners of public notebooks with paid subscriptions can view usage analytics in the analytics tab. [Learn more about Usage Analytics](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16206563).

Only notebook owners and editors can generate a public share link to a notebook and manage its public access. As an owner or editor, you can turn off public sharing by revoking public access  in the sharing panel. Any changes made by owners and editors will show up in shared public notebooks without needing to regenerate a link.

Once a public notebook is deleted or made private, any previously generated public share link will no longer work.

**Tip**: to confirm if your notebook is public, you will see a globe icon next to the Share button.

### How to visit public notebooks

Once you open the URL link, the public notebook will show on your homepage. You can interact with a public notebook by asking it questions or viewing content generated by owners or editors, such as audio overviews, FAQs or briefing documents.

To remove a public notebook from your homepage, select “Remove notebook” from the three dots menu.

### How to disable public sharing

1. As an owner or editor, open a publicly shared notebook
2. Select the “Share” button in the top-right corner of your notebook
3. Set Notebook Access back to Restricted.

Once public sharing is disabled, new visitors to previously generated links will no longer be able to access your notebook. Previous visitors will no longer see your notebook available in their shared notebooks page.

## Featured Notebooks

**Important**: the “featured notebooks” tab is only enabled for consumer accounts. Workspace Enterprise or Education accounts can only access featured notebooks through URLs.

Featured notebooks are designed to help you engage deeply with curated knowledge on a range of topics. These notebooks are pre-loaded with highest quality sources curated by authors, researchers, publications, and nonprofits around the world, covering topics from in-depth scientific explorations to practical travel guides to advice from experts.

### How to browse a featured notebook

You can browse featured notebooks in the “Featured notebooks” tab.

As a viewer, you can read the original sources, ask questions about them in chat mode, or consume pre-formatted artifacts like Audio Overviews or Study Guides. You will not be able to generate your own artifacts.

NotebookLM will use only these materials (and your current chat history) to answer questions or generate summaries. You won’t be able to add your own sources, but you can select or deselect specific sources in the left-hand source panel.
