# Try Document AI Stay organized with collections Save and categorize content based on your preferences.



Use the following demo to return annotations for your document file.

* Maximum file size is 20 MB.
* [File types](/document-ai/docs/file-types) accepted: PDF, GIF, TIFF, JPEG, PNG, BMP, WEBP.
* [Maximum pages](/document-ai/quotas#processor_limits) vary per processor.
* [Supported languages](/document-ai/docs/languages) vary per processor.
* Your browser must have JavaScript enabled.

**Note**:
The output generated on this try-it page is for illustrative purposes only and
might not reflect your results. The actual output might depend on the specific
model selected and any additional training applied to it. This demonstration is
for a general idea of product capabilities. For accurate and tailored results,
use our full suite of tools, and customize your model accordingly.

Upload a document or use one of the sample documents to get structured
document data.
